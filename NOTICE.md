## Independently consulted dictionary facts

Version 3 also consults individual modern facts in the Online Nahuatl Dictionary,
Stephanie Wood, editor, Wired Humanities Projects, University of Oregon:
https://nahuatl.wired-humanities.org/. The modern IDIEZ contributions are
distinguished from the historical and other fields in the same dictionary.
The university's project report identifies the contributed IDIEZ dataset as
modern Eastern Huastecan Nahuatl; individual entries do not always identify a
particular community or speaker:
https://blogs.uoregon.edu/wiredhumanitiesprojects/projects/indigenous-language-dictionaries/nahuatl-dictionary-report-for-year-2/.

These references support specifically reviewed form-and-sense facts. Itzli
authors its own short bilingual labels and usage notes. The dictionary's
authored definitions, examples, collection arrangement and media are not
imported or licensed by implication. CC BY-SA 4.0 applies to Itzli's original
contributions, not to the consulted dictionary or its assets. Exact entry URLs,
field locators, regional limits and review hashes accompany the published facts.

Individual reference facts may also be checked in *Tlahtolxitlauhcayotl:
Chicontepec, Veracruz* (Warsaw, 2016; ISBN 978-83-63636-51-7), by John Sullivan,
Eduardo de la Cruz Cruz, Abelardo de la Cruz de la Cruz, Delfina de la Cruz de la
Cruz, Victoriano de la Cruz Cruz, Sabina Cruz de la Cruz, Ofelia Cruz Morales,
Catalina Cruz de la Cruz and Manuel de la Cruz Cruz. Copyright notices identify
the authors, IDIEZ and the University of Warsaw. No explanatory prose, exercise
sets, dictionary selection/arrangement, layout or assets are imported. Brief
individual forms and minimal linguistic frames are identified at their exact
source heads; Itzli authors the practice scenes and tasks surrounding them.
Its IDIEZ source relationship to the online
dictionary is disclosed; two access routes do not establish independent consensus.

## Course-only database and private research boundary

The release database is `fcn_course_release.sqlite`, prepared offline from the
local research database and checked against every course query for all 43
legacy units. It contains selected course records, the applicable licensed
COERLL expressions, and an ID-only ledger for retired vocabulary. Existing
word and lesson IDs are preserved; learner account and progress records are
not copied into this file. Its manifest is `course-release-manifest.json`.

The full Kaikki/Wiktionary and Siméon corpora, raw dictionary entries and
non-runtime research prose are excluded. Legacy course records retain 17
Kaikki-linked identities for individually checked short language facts;
those identities do not expose the imported full definitions. Kaikki and
Wiktionary contributors are acknowledged for that historical research
lineage. This course-only release does not grant redistribution rights to
the retained private research database. A future full-corpus download needs
the source-entry attribution, applicable licenses and change notices first.
See `docs/course-only-release.md` for scope and compatibility checks.

## Alphabet reference

Display spelling follows INALI's Spanish-language **Alfabeto del idioma
Nawatlahtolli (náhuatl)** poster:
https://site.inali.gob.mx/INALIDhuchlab/assets/files/cartel_alfabeto_Nawatlatolli-espanol.pdf.
It has no printed publication date. The PDF's modification metadata is
January 31, 2025; INALI's listing page is dated May 28, 2025:
https://www.inali.gob.mx/detalle/lengua-nawatlahtolli-nahuatl.
The published poster is not marked draft. Its alphabet does not establish
that a complete writing norm has been finalized. Regional evidence still
determines the course's EHN words and grammar.
