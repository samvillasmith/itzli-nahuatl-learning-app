## Four checks in each lesson

1. **Word:** play a word, replay it and compare its audible form with its displayed text. Listen particularly to x/sh, w/kw, double l, h, stress and the selected airy tl approximation. Keep synthetic and native recordings distinguishable; Air B is an approximation, not a certified lateral affricate.
2. **Sentence:** hear the word inside a full sentence. Check person, object, tense, word endings and the exact sentence shown. Source recordings must remain the original clip for that text.
3. **Grammar and construction:** try the available matching, grammar answer or sentence builder. Hear the presented form or chunk, make one deliberate wrong answer, then hear the completed correct answer. Component cues must stay labeled as pieces rather than invented standalone words.
4. **Conversation:** fill the missing words, check the answer, then replay the completed speaker turn and reply. Replay another turn, retry a missed answer and confirm audio playback neither selects an answer nor changes an assessment score.

Some original lessons use a different activity sequence; apply each check wherever that activity is present. Record the lesson, exact written form and failure through the report control. Note absent playback, wrong words, truncation, silence, distortion, unusual pauses, a confusing source/synthetic label or an answer revealed before grading.

## One cache and offline check

Open an expanded lesson with **synthetic practice audio**, let its recordings finish saving, and replay one word and one completed sentence. Then take the browser offline and replay those same generated clips, including moving the audio seek control into the middle of a sentence. They should play from the device cache; unavailable storage or an unfinished download must be reported honestly. Return online afterward.

Original COERLL recordings remain external. Their preparation uses the browser's HTTP cache, whose retention and offline availability the browser controls; test them online and do not treat an offline failure as proof that a native recording was replaced. The test must not access a production account, clear existing progress or create new media.

The prior selected-form song hold is resolved through the owner-approved attested wikatl form, preserving ID 680 and lesson 31; the old mismatching clip stays withheld. Productive-context and other source/meaning limits remain explicit. A successful sample checks software behavior and audible usability in these cases; it does not establish that all pronunciation, every word sense or every spontaneous conversation has been independently verified.
