## New synthetic recordings and original recordings

New practice audio uses **Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, es-US-Standard-A**, at speaking rate **0.82**, pitch **0**, with mono **24 kHz LINEAR16** masters and **96 kbps mono 24 kHz MP3** delivery files. The selected **Air B** approximation uses X-SAMPA **tx**, a t followed by Spanish /x/ frication. It remains an approximation rather than certified native /tɬ/ pronunciation. The other reviewed Nahuatl pronunciation adjustments remain part of the recipe.

The current reviewed plan retains **644 distinct original source audio URLs** on the COERLL host: **545 exact-utterance recording URLs** plus **99 other passage or audio-asset URLs**. These are linked, unchanged source recordings, with their original notices. They were not copied into the S3 audio release, spliced into synthetic clips, used to clone a speaker or submitted as training material by this release pipeline. The pipeline uses an existing Google synthesis service and does not train a voice model; this does not establish what Google's underlying models were trained on.

Exact source recordings retain playback priority for their matching text. Synthetic clips are labeled as practice voices. Generated files can be stored in the app's validated device cache; preparation of external original recordings uses browser-managed HTTP caching and does not guarantee durable offline access.
